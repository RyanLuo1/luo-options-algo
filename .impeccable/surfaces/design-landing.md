# Surface brief — Luo Capital landing page (design/landing/v1…v5)

Scope: five committed directions of one Persuade surface (marketing landing page), each a self-contained `index.html` + `NOTES.md`. Visitor mode: Persuade. Build path: code-led (no image generation available). Hero imagery deferred: every version reserves a flat CSS stand-in at the exact slot and aspect the brief names.

Audience and job: an options trader who finds the category overwhelming lands here and must think "I can actually understand this" within three seconds, feel curious not intimidated, and run a first scan. Primary CTA "Run a free scan" in the hero and at the end. Secondary "See how it works". Proof: the product's own mechanism (transactable bid/ask pricing, probability, collateral, downside in plain dollars) shown as real components: one sample setup card and one ranked results table per version. No returns claims, no testimonials, no urgency.

Constraints (brief-pinned, all versions): generous whitespace, one focal point per section; light ground, two or three colors plus neutrals, one radius rule; distinctive display face plus clean body face; plain-English headlines; strategy explained visually in ≤3 steps; "what this is and isn't" moment; hints at paid tier (Picks, Performance) without claiming returns. Never: dark terminal, candlestick chaos, dense tables above the fold, get-rich imagery, stock photos, 3D blobs, icon-grid feature rows, purple-gradient SaaS, fake proof.

Seed: concept-seed key e1680c79 (mode persuade). The roll assigned index 3 and dealt six catalog challengers; the brief pins five complete directions, so the pinned directions govern and no challenger was fused or presented. Recorded for provenance only.

## Direction contract — v1 Soft Fintech Cards
THESIS: the trade is a stack of three cards you can hold; refuses the dense-terminal arrangement. OWN-WORLD: pale warm lilac-gray ground, white cards with one large radius (24px), soft offset shadows, black ink, flat violet accent, chartreuse reserved for the primary action; display Bricolage Grotesque, body Figtree. STORY: "a calm place where the credit is real" → runs a scan. FIRST VIEWPORT: nav; headline left with sub and two CTAs; right column a stacked sample-setup card cluster over a 4:3 hero slot. FORM: pinned direction 1 of 5, seed e1680c79. FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

## Direction contract — v2 Illustrated & Playful
THESIS: options explained like a friendly comic; refuses the fintech-card sameness. OWN-WORLD: flat illustration language with 2px black outlines, rounded geometric shapes, violet + chartreuse two-color palette on a soft light ground, radius 20px; display Unbounded, body Rubik; copy conversational. STORY: "oh, it's three moves" → curious → scans. FIRST VIEWPORT: full-width 16:7 illustration band behind the headline; headline on a soft white panel overlaid; CTAs inside the panel. FORM: pinned direction 2 of 5, seed e1680c79. FINISH: as above.

## Direction contract — v3 Calm Editorial
THESIS: a well-designed explainer, not an app; refuses product-shot heroes. STORY: reads, understands, trusts the honesty, scans. FIRST VIEWPORT: big statement headline, sub, CTAs; 21:9 full-width hero slot below. FORM: pinned direction 3 of 5, seed e1680c79. FINISH: as above.
